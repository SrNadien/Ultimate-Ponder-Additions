# Ex Deorum

A skyblock starts with nothing, and Ex Deorum is the mod that turns nothing into everything. Four
blocks do the work: a Barrel, a Crucible, a Sieve and whatever you are hitting things with.

The loop is always the same. Break stone down, sift what falls out, smelt what you find.
