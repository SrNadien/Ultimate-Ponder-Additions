# Sieve

Everything you mine on a skyblock comes out of a sieve, one handful at a time.

<GameScene zoom="5" interactive={true}>
  <ImportStructure src="../assets/exdeorum/sieve.snbt" />
  <BlockAnnotation x="3" y="1" z="2" color="#ddc166">Compressed Sieve</BlockAnnotation>
</GameScene>

Fit a mesh first, then put in dirt, gravel, sand or dust and click to sift.

The mesh decides what can drop. String finds seeds and the basics; flint, iron, gold, diamond and
netherite each open up more. Your first string comes from silkworms, which is why the game gives you
an advancement for crafting a string mesh.

A Compressed Sieve takes a compressed block and processes the whole thing in one go.

Sieves standing side by side all sift together when you drag the click across them, so build a row
rather than one at a time.
