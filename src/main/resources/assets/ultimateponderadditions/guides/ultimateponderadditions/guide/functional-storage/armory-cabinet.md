# Armory Cabinet

Drawers refuse anything with NBT, which rules out tools, armour and enchanted books.

<GameScene zoom="5" interactive={true}>

Hundreds of ordinary slots, happy to hold gear. Being a plain inventory, hoppers and pipes fill and
empty it as usual.
