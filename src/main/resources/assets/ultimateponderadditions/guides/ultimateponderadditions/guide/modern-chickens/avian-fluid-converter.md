# Avian Fluid Converter

Cracks Liquid Eggs into an internal fluid tank and sends the stored fluid to adjacent tanks or pipes.

<GameScene zoom="4" interactive={true}>
  <ImportStructure src="../assets/chickens/roost_generator.snbt" />
  <BlockAnnotation x="3" y="1" z="2" color="#5f6caf">Avian Fluid Converter</BlockAnnotation>
</GameScene>

Which fluid you get depends on the chicken that laid the egg, so a Lava Chicken farm ends up being a
lava supply.
