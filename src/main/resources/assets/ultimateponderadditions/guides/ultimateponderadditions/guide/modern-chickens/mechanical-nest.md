# Mechanical Nest

Uses a Robot Rooster and RF to maintain an aura that boosts nearby active Roosts and Mechanical
Roosts.

  <ImportStructure src="../assets/chickens/breeder.snbt" />

</GameScene>

Same job as the [Nest](nest.md), different fuel: power and a Robot Rooster instead of a live rooster
and a seed supply. Once you have RF this is the version that never runs dry.
