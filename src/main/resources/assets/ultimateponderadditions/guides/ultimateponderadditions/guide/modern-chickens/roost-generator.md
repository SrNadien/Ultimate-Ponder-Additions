# Roost Generator

Uses Redstone Flux Chickens to generate FE/t based on their count and stats, then exports the energy
to adjacent machines.

Unlike the Avian converters it does not process eggs. Chickens go in, power comes out, and the rate
depends on how many are inside and how good their stats are.

RF upgrades raise output, efficiency and buffer size.
